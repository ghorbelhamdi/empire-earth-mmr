How military stats affect MMR

Teammates start with the same result points. Winning against stronger opponents earns more. Personal rating uncertainty no longer gives one teammate a larger MMR bonus.

Confirmed military stats can adjust those points by up to 20%, rounded down to whole points. Adjustments balance within each team, keeping the team's total unchanged. Equal contributions and one-player teams receive no military adjustment.

Matches without stats use result points only. Open a match's MMR breakdown to see each player's result points, military adjustment, and final change. Historical matches show the rule used for their calculation.

Built for review

OCR runs on your computer. Captures stay local; only reviewed numbers and capture metadata go to the ladder.

Every companion report needs admin approval. Text recognition can misread names or digits, so your review is required. Military statistics do not identify the winning team.

If fullscreen capture is blank, use windowed mode or import a screenshot. This preview reads the English Military screen; manual entry works with other languages.
